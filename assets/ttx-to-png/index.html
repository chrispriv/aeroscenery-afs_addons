TTX / TSB / PNG / WAV Web Converter

Drop Aerofly FS .ttx textures or .tsb sounds. Decoding runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Drop a PNG or JPEG to write a plain .ttx (ETC2 for mobile, DXT for PC). Drop a .wav to write a .tsb (22050 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM, compress_file).

    Disclaimer: outputs are preview decodes of GPU/audio data inside the container — not a guaranteed copy of the artist’s original file. Textures may have been flipped, scaled, mipmapped, or block-compressed; sounds in Aerofly are 22050 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM. Re-encoding casually can look or sound wrong or crash the game. PNG → .ttx and WAV → .tsb are unofficial encoders. Textures are plain (compress_file off). Sounds are compress_file (LZHAM), which Aerofly needs in order to read mono16. Block formats are lossy. Prefer the IPACS converter for anything you ship.

    Textures: type_rgba, type_r, type_rg_rgtc2, DXT1/3/5, ASTC, ETC2(+EAC). Encode: ETC2, or DXT1 — DXT5 / ETC2 RGBA when the image has alpha. Sounds: .tsb ↔ WAV (mono16 at 22050 Hz, compress_file).
